FIG_03

A script made for listening

Amazon Bedrock writes a spoken lesson of 2 to 8 minutes. If Bedrock is unavailable, your notes are read aloud instead.

FIG_04

Two voices, one lesson

Amazon Polly records the MP3 and uses two distinct voices when you pick the two-host format.

FIG_05 / ARCHITECTURE

Serverless on AWS

One Lambda Function URL serves this page and the API. Uploads use presigned S3 URLs, a worker Lambda does the heavy lifting, and DynamoDB tracks each job. Files expire after one day and the app allows 100 podcasts per day.
